## 🌟 Overview

**FAMILYBRIDGE** is an AI-powered platform designed to help families navigate complex real-world situations.

When something unexpected happens — such as the loss of a family member, job loss, hospitalization, disaster, or financial difficulty — families often don't know:

- What they need to do
- Which organizations they need to contact
- Which documents are required
- What benefits or support may be available
- Which tasks should be completed first
- What information is missing

The problem is not always a lack of available support.

The problem is that **the systems are complicated and people don't know how to navigate them.**

FAMILYBRIDGE solves this by allowing users to describe their situation naturally.

Instead of asking:

> "Which department do you need?"

FAMILYBRIDGE asks:

> **"Tell us what happened."**

Gemini understands the user's situation, connects it with the family's available information, identifies relevant systems, detects missing information, and generates a prioritized action plan.
